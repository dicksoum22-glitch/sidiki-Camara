Discover the Land of Palestine

Palestine is a region rich in history, culture, and heritage, home to ancient cities, olive groves, and communities whose traditions stretch back thousands of years. From the hills of the West Bank to the Mediterranean coast of Gaza, each governorate holds its own stories, landmarks, and ways of life. Explore the governorates below to learn more about this land.

Explore the Governorates

Palestinian Governorates

Al-Quds (Jerusalem)

A city sacred, Jerusalem is a historic city and an important religioous center. It is home to Al-Aqsa Mosque and the old city.

Al-Khaleel (Hebron)

A historic city known for its old town, traditional markets, and the ibrahimi Mosque.

Yafa (Jaffa)

A historic Mediterranean port city known for its old town, orange groves, and coastal views.

Khan Younis

A southern Gaza Strip governorate known for its agricultural land and local markets.

Rafah

Located at the southern tip of the Gaza Strip, bordering Egypt, with a long coastal strip.

Gaza

The largest city in the Gaza Strip, situated on the Mediterranean coast with a long urban history.
